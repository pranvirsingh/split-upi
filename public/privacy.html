splitupi — privacy policy

Short version: your data never leaves your device.

  • UPI IDs, names, amounts, and notes you enter are used only in your browser/app to generate QR codes and payment links. They are not sent to, or stored on, any server.
  • SplitUPI does not process or hold money, and never asks for your UPI PIN, OTP, card details, or banking passwords.
  • No accounts, no analytics SDKs, no advertising trackers in the app.
  • Payments happen entirely inside your own UPI app, under your bank's terms.

Questions: open an issue at github.com/pranvirsingh/split-upi.
